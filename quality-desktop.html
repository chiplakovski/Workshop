Varmak AB — Quality Management

Permissions, approvals and the audit trail are enforced in the database rather than on this screen: a non-conformance closes only on verified evidence and a named approval, a critical failure applies its hold from inside the same transaction, and the history cannot be edited afterwards. Two things are not here — the documents themselves, which have nowhere to be stored yet, and a cryptographic signature: an approval here is a named person and a date.
